How this works

  1. Paste your markdown into the box.
  2. Click Chunk it.
  3. Your text is split into boxes, one for each heading, paragraph, or list.
  1. Each box is one piece of your page.
  2. Do your big chunking first. Need to split a box? Click Back, add a blank line where you want the split, and then chunk it again. Going back erases any labels and edits you've made here.
  3. Use the dropdown on each box to change what it is (heading, list, paragraph). The preview shows how it will look.
  4. Spot a typo or vague link? Click the pencil to edit that box's content, then click the check mark when you're done.
  5. Fix every accessibility or best practice issue listed below. When the list says "Nothing to fix," you've cleared everything this tool checks for.
  1. This is a simulation of your finished page. The real page gets its look from Brightspace, so check it there too.
  2. Anything still listed under Things to fix will go out as it is. You can go back and fix it first.
  3. Click Download to save the page, then upload it to Brightspace.
